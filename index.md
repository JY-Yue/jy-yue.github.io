I am a second-year Linguistics PhD student at the University of Toronto. My research areas are psycholinguistics, semantics, and pragmatics. My main advisor is Prof. [Daphna Heller](https://tlpl.ling.utoronto.ca/), and I also work with Prof. [Dave Kush](https://davewkush.github.io/). Previously, I studied in the MPhil Linguistics program at UCL, advised by Prof. [Wing Yee Chow](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/~ucjtwyc/Home.html). I graduated from the University of Chicago with a BA in Linguistics and a BA in Psychology, where I worked with Prof. [Ming Xiang](https://lucian.uchicago.edu/blogs/mingxiang/).

I study meaning. A central question guiding my research is: Is there such a thing as "linguistic meaning", that is independent from and potentially enriched by context? Or is context part-and-parcel of meaning and we can never isolate meaning from the interlocutors' production and comprehension processes? To approach these questions, I study factors like communicative goals, informativity, and bias. Empirically, I am interested in polar questions, wh-dependencies, and negation.
